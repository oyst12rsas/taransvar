MASTER'S PROJECTS & RESEARCH

Work on a real cybersecurity problem.

TaraSec invites Master's students to research, build, attack and independently evaluate a collaborative approach to Internet security in a live experimental environment.

Explore projects Contact us
Firewall detects malicious traffic → Originating network identifies the unit → Risk information is shared → Other networks decide how to act

A LIVE TEST ENVIRONMENT

Students do not start with a PowerPoint idea.

The existing environment includes NetBird and WireGuard networks, Linux VMs and gateways, C/Perl/PHP/database components, firewall threat data, Cowrie honeypots and working threat-reporting mechanisms.

Projects can be theoretical, experimental, implementation-oriented, or focused on finding weaknesses. Negative findings are useful results.

PROJECT OPPORTUNITIES

Choose a problem worth investigating.

01

Challenge the TaraSec concept

Threat-model the architecture, identify attack paths and trust failures, compare it with existing approaches, and suggest redesigns where assumptions fail.

02

Design better risk signalling

Investigate reliable, secure and interoperable ways for networks to associate unit-level risk information with Internet communication, including in-band and out-of-band approaches.

03

Connect real firewalls

Integrate Cisco, Fortinet, iptables/nftables or other systems; normalize alerts; measure false positives; and demonstrate end-to-end reporting.

04

Honeypots + threat intelligence

Expand Cowrie integration, evaluate other honeypot/IDS systems, classify attacks and assess the quality of automatic reports.

05

Global threat identification

Study confidence, severity, aging, corroboration, reputation, false-report resistance and whether statistics, ML or AI provide the best assessment model.

06

Build and attack the test network

Deploy gateways and endpoints, generate controlled attacks, measure overhead and latency, test failure recovery and explore scaling limits.

MORE DIRECTIONS

The list is a starting point.

Other possible projects include IPv6 architecture, transparent/Layer-2 gateways, router hardware integration, security APIs, VPN participation for users without a participating ISP, privacy engineering and reduction of unwanted Internet traffic.

We can adapt scope and technical depth together with the student and supervisor.
